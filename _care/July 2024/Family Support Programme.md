<p>We are pleased to introduce our Family Support Programme for CCK division
seniors 65 years and above. Do sign up via the QR code or head down to
the Chua Chu Kang CC during opening hours if you require assistance. One
application per household. Successful applicants will be invited to a one
off supermarket shopping trip and be given vouchers worth $50 to spend.</p>
