<p>Welcome to Chua Chu Kang IT Centre, located in the heart of Chua Chu Kang
Community Club. We have been providing quality IT education for over 20
years, serving thousands of satisfied individuals and companies. Our courses
cover a wide range of topics, including basic computer skills to advanced
courses. We also offered courses in graphic and webpage design, PC maintenance
and networking, programming and creating HP apps, 2D/3D animation, video
editing, and more.</p>
<p>We are proud to be an authorized Certiport center, offering globally recognized
certification courses, including Microsoft Office Specialist. Our focus
on Microsoft Office applications, such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and
Access, is designed to help you master these essential tools and advance
your career.</p>
<p>Our experienced trainers are dedicated to providing you with an exceptional
learning experience, and we offer flexible scheduling options to fit your
busy lifestyle. Whether you're looking to upgrade your skills for personal
development or career advancement, we are committed to providing you with
the skills and knowledge you need to succeed in the fast-paced world of
IT.</p>
<p>Use your SkillsFuture Credit (SFC) to upgrade your skills with quality
programmes that are affordable. Begin your lifelong learning journey with
our self-development and skills upgrading courses at the IT Centre today!</p>
<p>At Chua Chu Kang IT Centre, we are passionate about delivering the best
IT education to our students. Contact us at <strong>67647426</strong> or <strong>67691694</strong> today
to learn more about our courses and start your journey towards IT excellence&nbsp;with&nbsp;us.</p>
