<p>The <a href="https://www.pa.gov.sg/" class="MuiTypography-root MuiTypography-inherit MuiLink-root MuiLink-underlineAlways css-17qunhl" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>People’s Association (PA)</u></a> has a
network of more than 2,000 grassroots organisations (GROs). They are managed
by volunteers appointed by the PA. Through their involvement in the GROs,
these volunteers help the PA achieve its mission of building and bridging
communities to achieve one people, one Singapore. They bring people of
different backgrounds together by organising a wide range of programmes
for residents to meet and interact, encouraging community involvement,
raising awareness about community issues, explaining government policies
and gathering feedback, as well as helping those in need, so as to strengthen
racial harmony and social cohesion in our community.
<br>
<br>We welcome you to join us as a grassroots volunteer, and help build a
caring community and great home for everyone.</p>
